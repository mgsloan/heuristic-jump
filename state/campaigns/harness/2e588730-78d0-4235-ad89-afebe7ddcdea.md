## Target

Three gaps, all in the campaign-lifecycle bookkeeping that `harness/hj` and
`harness/loop` share. Each is the only open gap in its section, so each closes
a section:

- `loops.md#branches-exist-for-one-commit-at-a-time[967e89de52]` — a rebase
  that turns the gate red is escalated as a blocked item. `harness/loop:143`
  logs `hj intervene --kind merge-blocked`, and `merge-blocked` is in neither
  `INTERVENTION_KINDS` nor `KINDS_IN_USE`, so `cmd_intervene` dies and `|| true`
  swallows it.
- `loops.md#7-progress-stall-and-the-ways-it-is-faked[3dbde1bbd3]` — on stall
  the loop stops and writes `state/handoff.md`. Nothing writes it, and the
  prompt's stall notice fires on the same predicate that stops the loop, so
  under the single-campaign runner the notice is unreachable.
- `loops.md#5-the-auditor-and-the-conformance-loops-number[02f06f3aad]` —
  attribution is by named gap, not by count delta. `settle_progress` reads
  `named = bool(targets & audited_sections) and bool(closed_ids)`, a round-wide
  non-empty closed set, so a campaign that closed none of the gaps it named is
  credited whenever a sibling closes one.

**Shared context**, which is the test for taking three: all three are read out
of `harness/hj` and `harness/loop`, all three are the *stopping* machinery —
what counts as progress, what happens when there is none, and what happens when
a merge blocks a worker. `settle_progress` and the handoff both hang off
`trailing_without_progress`; `merge-blocked` and the stall stop both go through
`hj intervene`.

## Hypothesis

All three are the same shape my last campaign found in §11 and §12: not a wrong
word, a mechanism whose *cheap half* was deferred with its expensive half. The
handoff was deferred as "the campaign writes prose" and so nothing writes it;
the attribution was deferred to a round-wide approximation because the exact
version needs an id-to-section mapping the gap log does not carry; the
intervention kind was never added because the only call site swallows its own
failure. Each has an exact mechanical form available today.

## Outcome

**Confirmed.** All three gaps closed, one commit each, no reverts. `hj
selftest` 92 → 101.

The hypothesis held in all three: each gap was a mechanism whose cheap
mechanical half had been deferred along with an expensive one.

- `[967e89de52]` — `merge-blocked` was unusable for as long as it existed,
  because its only call site swallowed its own failure. The durable part is the
  new scan: a vocabulary enforced at runtime whose callers are shell scripts
  needs the shell scripts read, or the two drift in silence. The same scan now
  covers `"$hj" <subcommand>` for the same reason.
- `[02f06f3aad]` — the exact attribution needed one new field, because a gap id
  is `sha256(section|claim)` and a closed gap is no longer in the audit to look
  up. Rule 3 moves the attributed term on 13 of 50 historical campaigns and the
  progress verdict on none. `harness-010` records the redefinition.
- `[3dbde1bbd3]` — the handoff needed the loop to run one more campaign rather
  than write prose it cannot know. That made the previously unreachable stall
  notice reachable, which was the second half of the same gap.
