  attribution is by named gap, not by count delta. `settle_progress` reads
  `named = bool(targets & audited_sections) and bool(closed_ids)`, a round-wide
  non-empty closed set, so a campaign that closed none of the gaps it named is
  credited whenever a sibling closes one.

**Shared context**, which is the test for taking three: all three are read out
of `harness/hj` and `harness/loop`, all three are the *stopping* machinery —
what counts as progress, what happens when there is none, and what happens when
a merge blocks a worker. `settle_progress` and the handoff both hang off
`trailing_without_progress`; `merge-blocked` and the stall stop both go through
`hj intervene`.

## Hypothesis

All three are the same shape my last campaign found in §11 and §12: not a wrong
word, a mechanism whose *cheap half* was deferred with its expensive half. The
handoff was deferred as "the campaign writes prose" and so nothing writes it;
the attribution was deferred to a round-wide approximation because the exact
version needs an id-to-section mapping the gap log does not carry; the
intervention kind was never added because the only call site swallows its own
failure. Each has an exact mechanical form available today.
