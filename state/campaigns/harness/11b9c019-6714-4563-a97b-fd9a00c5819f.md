## Hypothesis

The cost machinery was built to the *shape* of section 15 and stops short of
three specific claims in it.

1. The row is missing `experiments (committed / reverted / empty)` and
   `gate seconds`. Both are named in the row spec; neither is in
   `state/cost/core.jsonl`. The experiment mix is the health signal the section
   says it keeps — "a campaign that is mostly empty experiments is stalling
   regardless of what it spent" — and nothing computes it.
2. Only the first budget scope exists (`budget_usd` per loop, passed through to
   `--max-budget-usd`). There is no per-language and no global ceiling, so
   "each stops and reports rather than continuing quietly" holds for one of
   three.
3. The derived number on the dashboard is output tokens per *campaign that
   progressed*. Section 15 asks for tokens per **gap closed** at 1a and 2b. The
   gap ledger already computes `gaps_closed`, so the specified ratio is
   available and is not the one being shown.

Expected change: all three are computable from what is already recorded, with
no new data collection, and the 30 existing campaign rows are backfillable from
their teed transcripts.
