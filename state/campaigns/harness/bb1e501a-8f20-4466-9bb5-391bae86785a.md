## Hypothesis

The last campaign's findings say the dashboard is *not* where the gaps are —
"all eleven panels render". That reading is about panels, and section 16 is
mostly not about panels: it names six specific fields for
`state/interventions.jsonl`, a full vocabulary of intervention `kind`s, three
derived numbers that "nothing else in the design reports", a twelve-field
`state/sessions.jsonl` row, and four properties of the transcript renderer.

So the hypothesis is that section 16 has the same shape section 15 had —
**named nouns the implementation approximated** — and that a panel-level
reading missed them because the panel exists and looks right. Specifically I
expect to find, and to close:

- `status` read from the decision file's frontmatter, though the section says
  the harness "derives the decision's status from the log rather than from
  someone remembering to write both";
- none of the three numbers section 16 says fall out of having the log
  (interventions per 100 experiments, recurring kinds, time-to-answer);
- no vocabulary for `kind`, which `hj intervene` takes as free text;
- `tokens` and `transcript path` missing from the `sessions.jsonl` row spec;
- the loop panel showing neither the current campaign's *hypothesis* nor its
  experiments so far, and having no `budget exhausted` state;
- the stall-handoff card showing `handoff.md` alone, where the evidence table
  asks for the campaign record and a journal excerpt too;

## Outcome

Confirmed. All seven of section 16's anchors were carrying gaps of exactly
the shape the hypothesis predicted — named fields and named ratios that the
implementation approximated — and every one of them was computable from
data already on disk. Nothing needed a new source of truth; what was
missing was arithmetic and one derivation.

The hypothesis was right for a reason worth carrying forward: the previous
campaign's finding, "the dashboard is not where the gaps are — all eleven
panels render", was a *panel-level* reading, and section 16 is mostly not
about panels. Six field names for `interventions.jsonl`, a vocabulary of
kinds, twelve fields in the session row, four properties of the renderer.
Panels are the cheapest thing in the section to check and the least of what
it claims.

The one thing that did not go as expected: deriving the decision status from
the log looked like a two-line change and turned into a third state, because
a human answered `harness-001` by editing the record while this campaign was
running. Neither "the log is authoritative" nor "the record is" survives
contact with that. See the journal.
