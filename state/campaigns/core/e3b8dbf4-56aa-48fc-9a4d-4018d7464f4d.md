## Target

`core.md#1-handler-interface` — "the handler seam trait lives in `shared`,
deliberately not `driver`, and dispatch is direct".

Picked over the other fifteen gaps because it is the one every other gap is
waiting on, which the previous campaign (`4ba19af5`) established and its
journal entry states outright: §2's snapshot constructor, §3's "no UTF-16 past
the edge", §4's `AbstainReason::NoCandidates` trigger, §5's dispatch hard-cap
and §7's trace record all need `LanguageHandler`, `Query` and `Outcome` to
exist before any of them can be more than a stub. The vendoring campaign
cleared the block it named — `vendor/rope` is on disk with `ByteOffset`,
`ByteLen`, `ByteRange` and `LineIndex` already defined — so the seam is
writable for the first time.

It is also the section with the largest fan-out per unit of work: §1 is one
section, but the types it defines are why twelve other gaps read "does not
exist anywhere in crates/".

## Hypothesis

The gap is not that the seam is hard to design — `core.md` §1 specifies it
down to field names. It is that the seam does not compile without a dependency
set `shared` does not have (`rope`, `tree-sitter`, `url`, `ignore`,
`thiserror`), and no earlier campaign was willing to add five dependencies for
types it was not also writing. Adding each one together with its first user is
what `deps.md` §14 asks for and is what makes this landable in one campaign.

Expected: `shared` holds the vocabulary types, `Error`, `Deadline`,
`DocumentSnapshot`/`SnapshotSeed`, `ProjectView`/`ProjectPath`,
`LanguageHandler`, `Query` and `Outcome`; `driver` holds a registry and a
direct dispatch call and depends on no language crate; a test asserts both
structural claims from `cargo metadata` rather than leaving them to be read.

## Outcome

`confirmed`. The seam is in `shared`, dispatch is a direct
`handler.goto_definition(&query)` behind a registry keyed by interned
`LanguageId`/`FileExtension`, and `crates/driver/tests/seam.rs` asserts both
halves of the claim mechanically — `type_name` for "defined in `shared`, not
`driver`", and the manifests for "no language dependency".

Two things stop short of §1's full text, both deliberate and both in the
journal: `ProjectView` has `roots`/`root_of`/`lookup`/`read` but not
`candidates`/`parse`/`scan`, and there is no per-query read cache. The second
is `conformance-005` — the shape `resolution.md` §3 describes is a lock, and
`CLAUDE.md` says to say so rather than add one.

One Class B escalation raised and taken provisionally (`conformance-004`,
`Location`'s field privacy) and one Class A spec fix applied
(CHANGE-conformance-003, the snapshot's `uri`).

The hypothesis held exactly: the section was blocked on a dependency set
rather than on a design question, and nothing in §1 needed to be decided that
`core.md` had not already decided.
