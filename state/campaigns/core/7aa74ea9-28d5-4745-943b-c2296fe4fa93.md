## Hypothesis

Every module `core` would own exists and says so in its own header —
`documents.rs`: "what is missing is the actor and the transport";
`trees.rs`: "what is missing is the channel"; `files.rs`: the same. The four
gaps are one missing owner, not four missing mechanisms, so one actor closes
all four and no part of it is speculative: each of its fields is a type that
already exists and already documents which method the actor was going to call.

The transport is *not* part of this. `shim.md` §2's codec, the child spawn and
the forwarding path are a separate and much larger piece of work, and they are
in a document this phase does not audit. What this campaign builds is the
state and the transitions; what stays missing is the wire that feeds them.

## Plan

1. Move `measure_core::record` to `shared::record`. `deps.md` §9's graph
   forbids `driver -> measure_core`, and §7 requires one record shape with two
   producers, so the type has to sit where both can reach it. `shim.md` §13
   keeps the *writer* in `driver` (`report/trace.rs`).
2. `driver/src/actor.rs` — `shim.md` §13's `actor.rs`: the state `core` owns
   and §7's four-step flow over it. It mints the deadline at arrival, records
   the pending query, dispatches, and resolves against the child.
3. `driver/src/trace.rs` — §7's JSONL writer, and the half-record a proxied
   query holds until the child answers.
4. `--trace=<path>` through `Cli` -> `Config` -> the actor.
