## Hypothesis

Both halves of the claim are implementable in phase 1a without a shim:

* The defaults are a pure function of the mode, and the mode is a pure
  function of whether a server command was given (`deps.md` §11: `server.is_
  empty()` *is* the mode). That is a `driver::config` module and a test that
  needs no I/O.
* The hard cap does not need a caller with a real handler. `Query` already
  carries the `Deadline`, so `dispatch` itself can drop a late `Outcome`, and
  the decision can be factored into a function over `(&Deadline, Dispatched)`
  that a test can drive with a `TestClock` — no `tree_sitter::Language`, which
  is what makes a full dispatch test impossible until `lang_rust` exists
  (journal, `e3b8dbf4`).

The risk is scope: `--deadline-ms` lives on a `clap` `Cli` in `heuristic_jump`
whose other flags (`--trace`, `--log`) belong to sections this campaign does
not target, and `main` has nothing to hand a resolved config to until
`driver::run` exists.

## Outcome

Confirmed. Both halves of the claim are implemented and tested:
`--deadline-ms` overrides, and absent it the mode's default applies — 750
proxying, 2000 standalone — with the mode being `server.is_empty()` and
nothing else; and `dispatch` drops a `Decided` whose deadline has expired.

Two shaping decisions worth keeping:

* The cap went **inside** `dispatch` rather than being left to the caller,
  reversing the comment the previous campaign left there. The deadline is not
  something a caller holds and `dispatch` does not — it is `query.deadline` —
  so putting it on the one path a handler is reachable by makes the claim a
  property rather than a rule. `call` is private; there is no uncapped route.
* A late *failure* is not converted to an expiry. Merging them would put a
  broken handler and a slow one in the same row of §7's table, which is the
  distinction `Dispatched` was split to keep.

Left undone deliberately, and named in the journal: `--trace`, `driver::run`,
and the `tracing` dependency that three crates now declare against a §9 graph
annotation that lists it for none of them.
