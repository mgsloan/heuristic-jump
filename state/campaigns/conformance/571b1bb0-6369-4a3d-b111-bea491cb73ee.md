## Target

`core.md#4-project-file-enumeration`, all three of its gaps:

- `bc6f2ede8e` — the driver caches a file list, built lazily on first need and
  refreshed in the background.
- `ccf81307a8` — `AbstainReason::NoCandidates` specifically triggers a

They are one piece of work seen from three sides: the three claims are the
*owner* of the file list and its two invalidation triggers, which §4 says
share one debounce rather than one each. Neither trigger can be written
without the owner, and the owner is pointless without them. Closing all three
is what makes the section clean, which is the only thing the number counts.

## Hypothesis

The gaps' stated blocker — "an owner that does not exist" — is not one. What
is missing is a struct in `driver` holding the `Arc<FileList>`, a named
background thread that walks, and a debounce; `shim.md`'s actor loop is *not*
needed for any of that, exactly as `TreeCache` did not need it (`core.md` §2
was judged clean with `Parsed` travelling by return value).

The two triggers reduce to O(1) state transitions on the same field, so "the
two triggers share one debounce" becomes a property of there being one field,
and "never reads the payload" a property of the tee taking no payload
argument at all.
