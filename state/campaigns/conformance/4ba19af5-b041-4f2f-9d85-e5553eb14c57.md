## Target

`core.md#vendoring-the-zed-crates` — an unjudged section, chosen over the nine
open gaps deliberately.

**Why an unjudged section, against the stated preference.** All nine open gaps
are blocked on the same missing artifact, and it is this one. `core.md` §1
defines the seam's text vocabulary — `ByteOffset`, `ByteLen`, `ByteRange`,
`LineIndex` — as *defined in* `vendor/rope` and re-exported by `shared`, "since
the dependency direction forbids the reverse". So:

- §1's `Location`/`Query` cannot be written: their fields are those types.
- §2's `DocumentSnapshot` holds a cloned `Rope`.
- §3's `WirePosition::resolve(enc, text: &Rope) -> Result<ByteOffset, _>` names
  the rope in its signature, and §3's whole argument for why the encoding risk
  is manageable is rope's `OffsetUtf16`/`PointUtf16` summary dimensions.
- §10's first two suites test §2 and §3.

Two of the audit's own `found:` lines say it outright — "vendor/ … does not
exist on disk" and "the re-exported rope newtypes are not expressible". The
remaining gaps (§4 file enumeration, §5 deadlines) each need a dispatch site,
which needs the seam, which needs this.

It is unblocked for the first time as of this campaign: decision
`conformance-002` was answered and a human placed the root `LICENSE-*` texts,
which `deps.md` §14 makes a precondition of the copy — `vendor/rope` arrives
carrying `LICENSE-GPL -> ../../LICENSE-GPL`.

## Hypothesis

The section is unjudged because nothing existed to judge. Its claims split in
two:

1. **Mechanical**: copy `rope` + `sum_tree` at `90d024b8` preserving symlinks,
   fold the three `util` items in, redirect `ztracing::instrument`, keep
   upstream tests and the benchmark, drop `tree_map.rs`, record everything in
   `vendor/README.md`, no `[lints] workspace = true` on vendored crates.
2. **Substantive**: the newtype rewrite of rope's public API, which the section
   says is "a design decision with its own re-sync cost and its own document:
   `rope-modifications.md`".

I expect to satisfy (1) and not (2) — a 51-function signature sweep across
7,400 lines is not one campaign, and `rope-modifications.md` is a document this
phase's audit does not cover. So the intended state change is
**unjudged -> gaps**, with the remaining gap being the newtype sweep, named
precisely enough that the next campaign can take it.

The number does not move this campaign. Nothing else moves it either until this
lands.
