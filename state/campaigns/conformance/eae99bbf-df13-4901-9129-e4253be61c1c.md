## Hypothesis

A provenance header nothing validates is worth nothing, and the two gaps are
the two halves of that: one field cannot distinguish two grammars, and no
field but `server_version` is compared on resume. The fix is to make the
header the thing a run is checked against — derive `grammar` from the
workspace lockfile so it names the revision actually linked, and refuse a
resume unless the header this run *would write* is the header already there,
field by field, with the comparison exhaustive so a new provenance field has
to be classified rather than silently unchecked.
