## Target

`core.md#vocabulary-types`, and its single open gap: `shared` re-exports four
of rope's seven text-shaped newtypes, so `shared::ByteColumn`,
`shared::Utf16Column` and `shared::CharCount` are not nameable by a crate that
may not depend on `rope` — which is the reason the section gives for the
re-export existing at all.

Picked by the stated rule — among gaps, the one whose section is closest to
going clean, because the number moves per section. This is the only section
with exactly one gap that is (a) in files that exist, (b) not blocked on a
crate nobody has written, and (c) not plausibly stale. `#the-trait` is also a
one-gap section but that gap is `ProjectView::{candidates, parse, scan}` — the
parse LRU and the bounded scan pool — which is both a whole campaign and

implement there; it wants a re-judge, not a campaign.

## Hypothesis

The gap is a leftover from campaign `bc8f02bb`, which targeted this section
and confirmed: the three missing names are the three the section itself
describes as the ones "handlers do not use", so they were the easy ones to
skip while writing the seam. Nothing depends on them being absent, and nothing
in `shared` has to change to add them.

The reason they went missing is that no compiler error could report it — the
re-export list is prose-checked. So the fix is two parts, and the second is
the one that lasts: add the three names, and put the assertion in a test that
lives in a crate which *does not depend on rope*, so the property under test
is the one the section states rather than a reading of the export list.
`driver` is that crate (`crates/driver/Cargo.toml` names `rustc-hash`,
`shared`, `tracing`), and `crates/driver/tests/seam.rs` already exists to hold
§1's other where-claims.
